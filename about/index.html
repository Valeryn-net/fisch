The idea

A cabinet of
aquatic curiosities.

Fischwiki is a personal, non-commercial project about fish. There are familiar river species here, open-ocean travellers and a few animals that look like someone got carried away.

The illustrations come mostly from the eighteenth-century work of Marcus Elieser Bloch. The species notes use modern names and describe how these animals live, reproduce and fare in the wild.

How to read the records

A note on the numbers.

Lengths and weights are reported maxima unless otherwise stated. Most fish you encounter will be smaller. Ages may be estimates or captive records; those are marked in the profiles. Standard length excludes the tail fin; total length includes it. A maximum is not a promise.

The conservation labels describe global IUCN assessments, which are published in different years. Atlantic cod’s widely cited Vulnerable assessment, for example, dates to 1996. Consult the linked record and local conservation guidance for a current decision.

Taxonomic placements can differ between authorities. Historical plate captions also use names that have since changed. These images are documents of natural history, rather than identification diagrams or scale drawings.
